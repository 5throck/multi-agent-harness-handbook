Core chapter

i18n Architecture and Multilingual Operations

Learn how to separate locale, translation, review, and delivery in a multi-agent environment.

In this chapter
  • Locale SSOT and scope
  • Division of roles
  • Parity and quality gates
  • Change and delivery

Locale SSOT and scope

Define the source for supported languages, default language, and display formats. Keep country profiles separate from language settings.

Division of roles

Translation transforms content, i18n review checks terminology, format, and omissions, and UI review checks layout. A role does not approve its own output alone.

Parity and quality gates

Automatically check source and translations for structure, links, code, and numeric tokens; people review meaning and cultural suitability.

Change and delivery

For a source change, identify affected locales and record translation, review, PR, and delivery state together. Treat a missing translation as a pre-release defect.

Practice: Record the decision, assign an owner, and verify the result before the next pipeline stage.
