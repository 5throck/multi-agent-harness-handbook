Guide for Graduates

A Guide for Graduates of the Introductory Course

This workshop (the Multi-Agent Team Harness Engineering Handbook, 2 days) is designed so that people brand new to "AI agent teams" and people who have already completed the introductory course (Claude Code & the Multi-Agent Harness) can take it together. If you graduated from the introductory course, some chapters will already feel familiar. This guide provides a skip map so you can get the most out of workshop time without repeating material you already know.

Reference: a chapter-structure mapping between the introductory handbook (intro-to-ai-harness) and this handbook (Multi-Agent Team Harness Engineering Handbook)

What This Guide Covers
  • A skip map that matches introductory-course chapters to this workshop's chapters
  • Which Day 1 sections you can skim vs. which you must attend in full
  • What you need in order to jump straight into Day 2 (IT Professional track)
  • A quick-start checklist

How to Use This Guide

Graduates of the introductory course would otherwise spend roughly 2–3 hours over the two-day workshop relearning material they already know. Using this guide's skip map, you can skim familiar sections and focus on what's actually new.

Facilitators should distribute this guide in advance and confirm each participant's background (introductory-course completion or not) before Day 1 begins, so group assignment and pacing can be adjusted accordingly.

Day 1: What Graduates Can Skip

Day 1 (general track) covers Chapter 2, Chapter 3 (guardrails), and Chapter 5 (hands-on practice with existing variants). For introductory-course graduates:

  • Chapter 2 §1 (harness concepts overview) can be skimmed — focus starting from §2 onward: lifecycle and template inheritance.
  • Chapter 3, Guardrails, covers material not addressed in the introductory course (permission models, the 3-tier risk classification, approval gates), so attend it in full.
  • Chapter 5, hands-on practice with existing variants, is split into 4 tool groups (Claude Desktop App / Claude Code / Antigravity Desktop / Antigravity CLI) — use the demos of tools you don't normally use as a chance to gain new insight.
  • The warm-up (Tetris → Pac-Man) only takes about 10 minutes, so joining in is low-cost. Treat it as an opportunity to observe how other groups think.

Day 2: Jumping Right In

Day 2 (IT Professional track) covers Chapter 7 (enterprise operating strategy), Chapter 8 (deep architecture), Chapter 9 (workflow patterns), Chapter 10 (L3 upgrade), Chapters 11–12 (creating and promoting a new variant), and Chapter 13 (capstone). This content has almost no overlap with the introductory course, so as long as you've completed the prerequisites, you can join directly on Day 2.

If you completed the introductory course, Chapter 8's L0→L1→L2 concepts will feel like review. The core of the new material is Chapter 8 §3 (AGENTS.md in depth), §4 (advanced architecture), and Chapter 10 (L3 upgrade).

Quick-Start Checklist

☐ You've completed this workshop's Setup Guide and Pre-Installation Checklist.
☐ Bring the artifacts you produced during the introductory course (agents, skills, team proposals) — you can put them straight to use in the Day 2 capstone.
☐ To jump straight into Day 2 (IT Professional track): confirm you have Claude Code or the Antigravity CLI installed and a GitHub account ready.
☐ Let the facilitator know whether you completed the introductory course, so it can factor into group assignment.
